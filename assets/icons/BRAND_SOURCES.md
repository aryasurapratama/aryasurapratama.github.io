These assets retain the shapes and colors supplied by their respective services. They identify outbound profile links; they do not imply endorsement.

| -------------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `google-scholar.png` | 48 × 48 px   | [Google Scholar favicon](https://scholar.google.com/favicon.ico), linked by the [Google Scholar homepage](https://scholar.google.com/)                                                                                                    | Largest favicon frame converted to PNG with macOS `sips`; geometry and colors preserved. |
| `sinta.png`          | 128 × 128 px | [SINTA favicon](https://sinta.kemdiktisaintek.go.id/authorverification/public/images/favicon_sinta.png?download=1), listed in the [official SINTA asset directory](https://sinta.kemdiktisaintek.go.id/authorverification/public/images/) | Downloaded original PNG without modification.                                            |

Retrieved and visually checked on 11 October 2026. SINTA serves its PNG when requested with its homepage as the HTTP Referer; the local copy avoids runtime dependence on that restriction.
