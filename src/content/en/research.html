2025Digital literacy & AI

Digital literacy and learning readiness in AI-assisted learning

EDUTECH · 5(4), 828–836

The study reports a significant relationship between digital literacy and deep learning in an AI context. Learning readiness makes a positive contribution that is not statistically significant in the study sample.

Methods & study context
Method
Quantitative survey; multiple linear regression.
Context
100 respondents from eight high schools in Bandar Lampung.
Authorship
Arya Sura Pratama · co-author

2024Mindfulness & learning

Mindfulness practices and the quality of classroom learning

International Journal of Science and Applied Science: Conference Series

This study examines the relationship between mindfulness practices and learning quality among junior high school students. The analysis reports a significant positive relationship within the schools and period studied.

Methods & study context
Method
Quantitative survey; simple regression.
Context
Population of 70 students; academic year 2023/2024.
Authorship
Arya Sura Pratama · co-author

2024Audiovisual media & Buddhist education

Audiovisual media and cognitive learning outcomes in Cattari Ariya Saccani lessons

International Journal of Science and Applied Science: Conference Series

The study examines audiovisual media and cognitive learning outcomes in lessons on Cattari Ariya Saccani. Its analysis reports a significant relationship in the sample of students studied.

Methods & study context
Method
Quantitative survey; validity, reliability, and simple linear regression tests.
Context
34 junior high school student respondents.
