Common questions.

How do I start a conversation?

Use WhatsApp for a direct conversation. Include your name, context, and purpose. The message builder on this page helps organize the initial information before opening WhatsApp.

What should I prepare for a project discussion?

Prepare the problem, intended users, essential features, existing systems, and timing. Requirements, visual references, or workflow examples can help the discussion.

How can I share a research idea?

Describe the topic, research question, study context, and the collaboration you would like to discuss. Include relevant publication links or a proposal summary if available.

Where can I find your academic profile and publications?

The Profile page links to Google Scholar, SINTA, and ORCID. Research provides publication summaries and publisher links; Education links to the master’s thesis.
