Stages of product development.

The following stages summarize the process described by SoftKlinik. Work details depend on project requirements and scope.

  1. 01

    Planning & design

    Understand objectives, users, and requirements, then discuss workflows and initial product designs.

  2. 02

    Development & integration

    Build interfaces and application functions, including connections to required systems or services.

  3. 03

    Testing & launch

    Check functionality and the user experience before preparing the product for use.

  4. 04

    Maintenance & development

    Discuss support after launch and subsequent improvements within the agreed scope.
