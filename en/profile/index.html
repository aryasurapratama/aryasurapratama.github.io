About me.

I am Arya Sura Pratama, M.Kom., a permanent lecturer at Jinarakkhita Buddhist College of Lampung, affiliated with the Buddhist Religious Education Study Program. My bachelor’s and master’s qualifications are in Computer Science.

I also develop websites and software and serve as Founder & Technology Lead at SoftKlinik. Academic and development work connect learning, scholarly inquiry, and the needs of digital systems.

Teaching Information and Communication Technology.

I teach ICT. The following themes describe the context of information and communication technology in education.

Digital devices & applications

ICT provides a context for understanding devices and applications and choosing tools suited to learning and academic work.

Information & digital literacy

Finding, managing, and evaluating information are important aspects of technology use. Digital literacy also connects with learning in environments that use AI.

Communication & collaboration

Digital communication involves sharing information, organizing collaborative work, and interacting responsibly in educational environments.

Three connected areas of work.

Education

ICT teaching places technology in a learning context, with attention to information and digital communication in academic activities.

Research

Co-authored publications examine learning and educational technology. My master’s thesis adds an IT governance and service management perspective.

Development

Web and software development through SoftKlinik brings attention to user needs, application functions, and relationships between systems.
