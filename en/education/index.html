IT governance in my master’s research.

My master’s thesis examines IT governance and service management at STIAB Jinarakkhita Lampung. It relates technology to academic and administrative needs and institutional objectives.

The study combines COBIT 2019 with ITIL v4 practices. Questionnaires, interviews, observations, and documents support an assessment of existing practices and recommendations for improvement.
